# 📖 Reader Section - Complete Guide

## 🎯 Overview

Bagian **Reader** adalah mode utama untuk pengguna yang ingin membaca series, mengikuti author, mengumpulkan XP, dan berinteraksi dengan komunitas. Reader memiliki 5 tab utama dengan 15+ screens.

---

## 📱 Navigation Structure

### **Bottom Navigation Bar** (5 Tabs)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🏠 Home  |  🔍 Discover  |  🏆 Ranking  |  📚 Library  |  👤 Profile  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **🏠 Home** - Feed utama dengan featured series dan recommendations
2. **🔍 Discover** - Search dan explore series
3. **🏆 Ranking** - Leaderboard global dan per-author
4. **📚 Library** - Koleksi personal (bookmarks, history, collections)
5. **👤 Profile** - Profile reader dengan stats dan settings

---

## 🏠 Tab 1: Home Screen

### **Fitur Utama:**

#### 1. **Header Section**

- **Greeting**: "Discover" / "Find your next favorite story"
- **Actions**:
  - 🔔 **Notification Bell** (dengan badge unread count)
  - 🎯 **Missions Icon** (quick access ke missions)
  - 🔍 **Search Icon** (quick access ke search)

#### 2. **Featured Carousel** (Auto-scroll)

- Menampilkan 3-5 series featured
- Auto-scroll setiap 5 detik
- Tap untuk buka series detail
- Indicator dots di bawah

#### 3. **Continue Reading**

- Series yang sedang dibaca (dari reading history)
- Menampilkan progress (episode terakhir)
- Quick resume reading

#### 4. **Trending Series**

- Series dengan views tertinggi minggu ini
- Horizontal scroll
- Cover + title + author + views

#### 5. **New Releases**

- Series baru yang baru dipublish
- Horizontal scroll
- Cover + title + author + "New" badge

#### 6. **Recommended for You**

- Personalized recommendations based on:
  - Genre preferences
  - Reading history
  - Liked series
- Vertical list dengan detail lengkap

#### 7. **Popular Authors**

- Author dengan followers terbanyak
- Avatar + name + follower count + tier badge
- Tap untuk buka author profile

### **Interactions:**

- **Pull to refresh**: Reload semua data
- **Tap series card**: Buka series detail
- **Tap author**: Buka author profile
- **Infinite scroll**: Load more recommendations

  - Avatar
  - Pen name

  - Follower count

- **Switch tabs**: Change library view
- **Tap series**: Open series detail

- **Create collection**: Add series to collection

## 🎮 Gamification Features (Reader-Specific)

### **XP System**

**Cara Mendapat XP**:

- 📖 Read episode: 10 XP
- ❤️ Like series: 5 XP
- 💬 Comment: 15 XP
- 👥 Follow author: 10 XP
- 🔖 Bookmark series: 5 XP
- 🎁 Daily reward: 10-50 XP (based on streak)
- 🎯 Complete mission: 20-200 XP
- 🏅 Earn badge: 50-500 XP

**Level System**:

- Level 1-100
- XP required increases per level
- Level up rewards (coins + badges)

### **Missions System**

- **Daily**: 4 missions, reset 00:00
- **Weekly**: 3 missions, reset Monday
- **Lifetime**: 10+ missions, one-time
- **Rewards**: XP + Coins
- **Auto-claim**: Optional

### **Badge System**

- **Categories**: Reader, Social, Collector, Special
- **Rarity**: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary
- **Total**: 30+ badges
- **Display**: Profile showcase (top 3)

### **Leaderboard**

- **Global XP**: All readers
- **Season-based**: Reset setiap season
- **Rewards**: Top 10 dapat bonus coins
- **Real-time**: Update setiap 5 menit
